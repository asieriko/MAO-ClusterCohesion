# Matrix Aggregation Operators

Aggregation theory has traditionally focused on operators defined over vectors. However, many applications—including Multi-Criteria Decision Making, Group Decision Making, Fuzzy Rule-Based Classification Systems, and overlap/grouping indices—require aggregating information naturally structured as a matrix of membership degrees (e.g., where a set of objects interacts with a family of fuzzy sets). Despite this, no formal framework has been proposed for this class of operators, partly due to the common practice of flattening matrices into vectors (which discards structural information) and partly due to a reliance on decomposable operators that aggregate rows and columns sequentially. This paper addresses this gap by formalizing the notion of a matrix aggregation operator (MAO). We analyze the decomposability and symmetry properties of MAOs, showing that certain operators cannot be expressed in decomposable form and examining several notions of symmetry. Finally, we introduce a family of MAOs combining grouping functions and MEOWA operators, termed Maximum Entropy Global Coverage Indices (MEGCIs), and illustrate their usefulness in cluster quality assessment through an extensive computational study. 

**Keywords:** Matrix Aggregation Operators, Symmetry, Decomposability, Global Coverage Indexes, Clustering

**highlights**
- Definition of a new family of aggregation operators: Matrix Aggregation Operators (MAOs).
- Study of symmetry and decomposability properties of MAOs.
- Several examples of MAOs in applications.
- Maximum Entropy Global Coverage Indexes (MEGCIs): MAOs combining grouping functions and MEOWA operators.
- Application of MEGCIs for cluster cohesion and quality assessment.
